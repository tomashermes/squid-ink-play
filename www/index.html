the Ink Flight makeover — flap through the coral tunnel

  • Press Space (or tap / Dive) to start — you glide safely for a heartbeat before the current takes hold, so no early falls and no accidental jumps.
  • Tap / click / Space — jet-propel upward. Every flap puffs harmless ink behind you.
  • Fly lazy: flap after flap at the same height, and each one casts a fisherman's net about two seconds ahead, exactly where you keep returning.
  • Translucent = still being cast and harmless; then the corks snap and it sets solid — a set net snags you like the rocks — before it frays away.
  • Catch a glowing fish on the way and it harpoons you forward — a strong surge, so gaining ground never depends on a perfect jump.
  • Rule of the squid: vary your height and you cast nothing. Dodge the coral tunnel… and your own habits.
  • P / Esc pauses — it also auto-pauses when you switch tabs.
